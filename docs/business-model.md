# Business Model: Risk and damage evaluation

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isic-6621`
- ISIC Rev.5: `6621`
- Activity: independent loss adjusting and risk evaluation -- damage appraisal and risk assessment performed for insurers or insureds, independent of underwriting or claims-payment authority
- Social impact: financial inclusion, data sovereignty, transparent audit

## Customer

- independent loss adjusters
- risk-engineering consultancies
- insurers and brokers outsourcing damage evaluation
- catastrophe-response adjusting pools

## Offer

- inspection intake and scheduling
- damage/risk evaluation proposal
- evidence-backed valuation report
- immutable audit ledger

## Revenue

- self-host setup: one-time implementation fee
- managed hosting: monthly subscription per adjuster seat
- support: monthly retainer with SLA
- migration: import from an incumbent adjusting platform
- per-inspection fee

## Trust Controls

- no valuation report is finalized or issued without human sign-off
- a fabricated jurisdiction methodology citation, an undisclosed
  conflict of interest on the assigned adjuster, or an unsupported
  valuation (required evidence not on file) forces a hold, not an
  override
- every intake, assessment, screening and finalization path is auditable
- emergency manual override paths remain outside LLM control
