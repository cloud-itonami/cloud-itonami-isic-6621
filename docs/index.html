Risk and Damage Evaluation

ISIC 6621 • Governed loss adjustment and independent valuation

An open-source, forkable business blueprint for independent loss adjusters and risk-evaluation consultancies. Built on a portable Clojure runtime with governed workflows, this system drafts damage and risk valuations backed by jurisdiction-specific methodology standards, checks for adjuster conflicts of interest, and ensures every valuation proposal routes through a Loss Adjustment Governor before human approval.

The core premise: an LLM excels at drafting checklists and spotting gaps, but it has no notion of which jurisdiction's standards are official, no way to verify an adjuster's independence, and no business finalizing a real settlement valuation. The Loss Adjustment Governor wraps the LLM in a separate, overridable audit layer that blocks fabricated methodologies, undisclosed conflicts, and unsupported evidence—making independence a system property, not an assumption.

Deploy as a self-hosted instance in your jurisdiction, configure your own valuation standards and conflict-screening rules, and let the immutable audit ledger prove every decision to regulators and customers alike.
